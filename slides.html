The Problem: The Deep-Space Clinical Vacuum & Cognitive Gap

NASA's 5 Hazards of Spaceflight

  • Space Radiation: Ionizing cosmic rays & solar particle storms.
  • Isolation & Confinement: Circadian disruption, monotony, stress.
  • Distance from Earth: Up to 22-min one-way speed-of-light comms latency.
  • Gravity Fields: Fluid shifts, cardiovascular deconditioning, SANS vision loss.
  • Hostile/Closed Cabin: Toxic trace contaminants, insidious hypoxia risk.

The Crew Reality: Trained, But Non-Specialist

  • Trained, Not Specialists: Astronauts and Crew Medical Officers (CMOs) receive cross-disciplinary medical training, but are pilots and engineers—not full-time ER physicians.
  • High Cognitive Load: Recalling dozens of complex differential diagnoses under physical exhaustion and microgravity fluid shifts is a major cognitive burden.
  • Latency Gap: Real-time teleconsultation collapses over 40-minute round-trip delays.
Sentinel's Role: An autonomous clinical co-pilot and cognitive force multiplier for the trained CMO.

The Clinical Intelligence Engine: Deterministic Safety

Why We Reject Black-Box LLM Diagnostic Models

In human spaceflight, probabilistic hallucinations can be fatal. Deterministic rules and NASA medical specifications make 100% of triage decisions. The LLM is strictly confined to natural speech rephrasing.

The 5-Layer Anomaly Pipeline

  • 1. Safe Reference Limits: Instant alerts (e.g. cabin $O_2 < 19.5\%$).
  • 2. Personal Baseline (MAD): Rolling median deviations detect subtle personal shifts.
  • 3. Multi-Day Slope Trends: Catches progressive decline over 3-5 days.
  • 4. Cross-Signal Patterns: Multi-system compounding alerts (Sleep ↓ + Reaction Time ↑ + Mood ↓).
  • 5. Contextual Suppression: Silences alerts post-exercise or during EVA.

The Brain LLM with Numeric Guard

  • Groq Llama 3 / Whisper STT + local fallbacks.
  • System prompt locks model to rephrasing JSON cards in at most two short sentences.
  • Regex Numeric Guard: Code verifies spoken numbers match the card exactly; prevents invented dosages.

In Sentinel, our 5-layer anomaly engine uses Median Absolute Deviation to detect personal drift.
